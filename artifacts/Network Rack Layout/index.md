## Summary

I learned how to design and organize a professional network rack the way real cybersecurity labs and data centers do it. This included planning the physical layout, mounting equipment, managing airflow, and separating power and network paths. I also learned how to connect and label patch panels, install switches and a router, and understand how each device fits into a secure, segmented network. Working on Step 3 of the rack build gave me hands‑on experience with enterprise hardware, structured cabling, and preparing the lab’s infrastructure for virtualization and future cybersecurity environments.

**Project:** cs Lab and rack setup

**My role:** I worked independently on setting up my workstation and personal cubicle in the computer lab. I also worked with Nate to build Step 3 of the server rack setup. Through this work, I gained practical experience with installing and organizing rack equipment and preparing the lab infrastructure for future use.
